學習目標：能描述商品問題並提出合理要求。

A: I'd like to return these headphones.
中文：我想退這副耳機。

B: What seems to be wrong with them?
中文：耳機出了什麼問題？

A: The left side doesn't work. I bought them yesterday.
中文：左邊沒有聲音。我昨天買的。

B: Do you have the receipt?
中文：你有收據嗎？

A: Yes, here it is. Could I get a replacement?
中文：有，在這裡。我可以換一副嗎？

B: Let me check our return policy and see what I can do.
中文：我查一下退換貨規定，看能怎麼處理。

句型解說

句型解說

［第 45 頁］
進階句型速查
完成式：I have finished my homework. 我已完成作業。Have you ever been there? 你去過那裡嗎？
時間比較：I visited Tainan last year. 指特定過去；I have visited Tainan. 強調迄今經驗。
since 起點：since Monday；for 長度：for three days。
比較：cheaper than / more useful than / as useful as。
建議：You should check the details. 你應核對細節。Why don’t we ...? 我們……如何？
條件：If it rains, we’ll stay home. 如果下雨，我們會待在家。
被動：Breakfast is included. 包含早餐。The room was cleaned yesterday. 房間昨天打掃過。
關係子句：The book that I borrowed is useful. 我借的那本書很實用。
修飾與原因：a useful book；speak clearly；because I’m busy；although it’s difficult。
動詞搭配：enjoy reading；want to read；ask someone to read；look forward to meeting you（to 在這裡是介系詞）。
動名詞：Reading helps me relax. 閱讀讓我放鬆。
常見修正：more better→better；discuss about it→discuss it；borrow me a pen→lend me a pen / let me borrow a pen。
